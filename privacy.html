Data I Collect

Heart Rate Data (HealthKit): slowfaster reads your real-time heart rate from Apple HealthKit to provide biofeedback during the experience. This data is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to any server.

Resting Heart Rate: The app may read your resting heart rate history from HealthKit to automatically determine your baseline. This data stays on your device.

Local Settings: Your preferences (sound settings, traffic side, resting heart rate) are stored locally on your Apple Watch using standard system storage. This data never leaves your device.

Data I Do NOT Collect

  • I do NOT have servers - all processing happens on your device
  • I do NOT collect analytics or usage data
  • I do NOT use cookies or tracking
  • I do NOT store or transmit your health data anywhere
  • I do NOT sell any data

HealthKit

slowfaster uses Apple HealthKit to access heart rate data. Apple requires explicit user permission before any health data can be read. You can revoke this permission at any time in your iPhone's Settings > Privacy > Health > slowfaster.

Per Apple's HealthKit guidelines, your health data is encrypted and protected by your device's security. slowfaster only reads heart rate data during active use and does not store health data beyond the current session.

Legal Basis (GDPR)

For EEA/UK users, my legal basis for processing is:

  • Consent: You explicitly grant HealthKit permission to share heart rate data with the app
  • Legitimate interest: Processing heart rate locally to provide the core biofeedback experience

Data Retention

Heart rate data is only used in real-time during active sessions and is not stored. Local settings remain on your device until you delete the app.

Your Rights

Under GDPR/Swiss law, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to processing
  • Withdraw consent (revoke HealthKit access)

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can exercise these rights by adjusting HealthKit permissions or deleting the app.

Security

Your data is protected by Apple's device encryption and HealthKit security infrastructure. Since no data leaves your device, there is no network transmission risk.

Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. I do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

California Residents (CCPA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, you have the right to know what personal information is collected and request deletion. Since slowfaster stores all data locally and has no servers, you have full control over your data on your device.
